Exhale is an open-source, self-hosted subscription manager where **the reminder is the interface**. The message that tells you Netflix renews in three days lets you fix it right there: mark it paid, move the date, change the card or the amount, cancel. The message updates in place, and so does the web.

It is one program with one data folder. No cloud, no account with us, no telemetry, no paid tier.

## How it works

1. **Install it** on a computer that stays on: a laptop, a Mac mini, a NAS, a Raspberry Pi or a small server. Exhale needs no public address: it reaches Telegram and Slack with outgoing connections only.
2. **Create your account** and follow the steps on the home page: create your own Telegram bot (guided), link your chat, add your subscriptions or import them from Wallos, a spreadsheet or your bank statement.
3. **Answer the reminders.** Each one carries the buttons that fit: already paid, a new date, another amount or card, skip, cancel, undo.
